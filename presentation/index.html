07 · Interpretation

Bright is not
always wet.

False colour is a physical measurement, not a photograph. Read the channels together, then subtract the surroundings.

R · HH

Co-pol

Surface roughness and dielectric change. Wet soil usually brightens HH; standing water can darken it.

−22 → −3 dB
G · HV

Cross-pol

Vegetation volume and canopy structure. It separates a wet field from a wet tree, and it is more sensitive to biomass.

−28 → −11 dB
B · RATIO

HH / HV

The ratio distinguishes surface scattering from volume scattering. It is the third channel in every chip.

3 → 12 dB
HOW TO READ A CHIP

Red = HH  ·  Green = HV  ·  Blue = HH/HV ratio. Shared stretch across the district images, the chips and the renderer.

THE NUMBER THAT MATTERS

Contrast = mean inside 30 m − mean in the 100–300 m annulus. It removes the weather that moved both.

inside 30 m+6.1
100–300 m+3.4
contrast+2.7

A 2026-09-21 → 10-03 background shift of +2.5 dB HH is exactly why raw brightness alone is not enough.

08 · Limits

A map of evidence.
Not a verdict.

The product is honest about its resolution, its labels and its privacy rules.

WHAT IT SHOWS

The record

  • 1,691 logged repairs, 2019–2026
  • 918 pipe segments and 7 pressure zones
  • 24 NISAR passes over the district
  • 703 baseline / before / after chips
  • Contrast against a 100–300 m annulus

Read as: where to inspect first.

WHAT IT DOES NOT

The claim

  • It does not confirm a leak
  • It does not localise to a metre
  • It does not separate every wet field from a leak
  • It does not have radar before 2025-10-17
  • It does not use a trained detector

Read as: evidence, not proof.

PRIVACY BUILT IN

The people

  • Staff account names are never read
  • Notes that name people are withheld
  • Test record 1611 is dropped
  • Raw loss text is never shown
  • Meter and parcel layers are not used

Read as: privacy by default.

The right output is a place to look first, and a clear statement of uncertainty. 10 m chips · 20 m district images · 30 m stats disc
