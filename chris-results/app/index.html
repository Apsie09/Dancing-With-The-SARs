About TerraGuard

TerraGuard is a NASA Space Apps 2026 “Dancing with the SARs” project. It combines weather-normalised Sentinel-1/2 and Landsat evidence with pipe networks and repair records.

It is a screening and triage tool, not a leak detector. The final score ranks places where the surface stays wetter than weather and land cover predict; it does not prove a leak.

Built from the team’s TerraGuard pipeline, the Kitchener/NISAR data work, and the Coryell NISAR viewer. The code and case notes are in the chris-results/ bundle.
